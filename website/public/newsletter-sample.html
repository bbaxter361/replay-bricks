🧱 Brian's Corner

Thoughts from Brian

Welcome to another edition of The Brick Bulletin. This week I want to talk about something I've been watching closely: the shift in the Botanical Collection market. For years, investors slept on these sets. "They're just plants," people said. Well, those plastic plants have been printing money.

The numbers don't lie: 10311 Orchid and 10309 Succulents have averaged 45% appreciation within a year of retirement. That's not just good — that's exceptional. And now with 10349 Lotus Flowers hitting pre-orders with massive demand, it's clear this theme has legs. The Botanical sets have something licensed themes don't: universal, timeless appeal. No movie fatigue, no reboot controversy. Just beautiful builds that look stunning on a shelf.

But here's my contrarian take: the real opportunity right now isn't in the Botanicals you already know about. It's in the retiring Architecture sets. 21056 Taj Mahal is entering its final production run at $119.99 MSRP — and historically, Architecture landmarks double within 6 months of retirement. The Taj Mahal is an iconic build with massive international appeal. If you're only buying one set this month, that's the one.

What am I avoiding? Overhyped pre-orders on unproven themes. The FOMO is real, but patience is what separates the good investors from the great ones. Wait for the data. Wait for the retirement confirmation. Then strike.

As always — bricks only go up. But some go up faster than others. Pick wisely.

— Brian
